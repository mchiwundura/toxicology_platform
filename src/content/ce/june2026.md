# Speaker Background

Dr Margaret Siyawamwaya is a lecturer in Pharmaceutics at the University of Zimbabwe and a registered pharmacist with a strong interest in paediatric pharmacy and medication safety. Her research focuses on improving access to paediatric-specific medicines in Africa, with particular emphasis on safe and effective medicine use in children.
