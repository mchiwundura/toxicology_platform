# Speaker Background

Hlengiwe Moyo is a pharmacist. she holds a Bachelor of Pharmacy and Master of Pharmacy degree from Rhodes University. With a background in research, Hlengiwe has a strong interest in the intersection of pharmacy practice and public health She has delivered presentations on her work at both regional and international forums contributing to critical discussions on pharmacy’s evolving role in global health.
