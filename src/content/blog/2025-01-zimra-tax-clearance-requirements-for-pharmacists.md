The Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA) has introduced amendments under Section 80A of the Income Tax Act [Chapter 23:06], which mandate the submission of a valid Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC) for professional registration and licensing across various professions, including pharmacists. These requirements are part of ZIMRA's drive to enhance compliance and streamline tax administration. Here's what pharmacists need to know about these changes and how to navigate them efficiently.

## What is a Tax Clearance Certificate?

A Tax Clearance Certificate is an official document issued by ZIMRA to confirm that a taxpayer is compliant with tax obligations. The certificate shows that the individual or entity has filed all required tax returns and paid any outstanding taxes.

## Why is a TCC now required for pharmacists?

The new policy applies to health practitioners, including pharmacists, registered under the Health Professions Act [Chapter 27:19]. The aim is to ensure that all practising professionals are tax compliant at the time of registration, renewal, or certification with the Health Professions Authority (HPA) or relevant professional councils.

This requirement aligns with broader efforts by ZIMRA to formalise professional sectors and curb tax evasion.

## When should the TCC be submitted?

Pharmacists must provide a valid TCC at two key points:

1. **During registration:** If you are a new pharmacist registering with HPA or another professional body for the first time.
2. **At renewal:** When renewing your professional licence.

The TCC must be valid and issued no earlier than 30 days prior to submission to the licensing body.

## Steps to obtain a TCC

1. **Registration on the ZIMRA platform:** Pharmacists must register for a Business Partner Number (BPN) via the ZIMRA Self-Service Portal (SSP) at [mytaxes.zimra.co.zw](https://mytaxes.zimra.co.zw).
2. **Submission of tax returns:** Ensure that all required tax returns (income tax, VAT, and PAYE, if applicable) have been filed.
3. **Payment of outstanding taxes:** Settle any tax arrears or negotiate a payment plan with ZIMRA.
4. **Application for the TCC:** Once compliant, apply for the TCC through the SSP.

## Consequences of non-compliance

Failure to submit a valid TCC can lead to:

- Rejection of registration or licence renewal applications.
- Suspension of professional practice rights.
- Additional penalties and interest on outstanding tax obligations.

## Additional notes for pharmacists

1. **Collaborate with your employer:** If you are employed, confirm with your employer that they are compliant with PAYE and VAT remittances, as this can affect your individual tax status.
2. **Plan ahead:** Apply for your TCC well before your registration or renewal deadline to avoid delays.
3. **Consult a tax expert:** If you encounter challenges, seek assistance from a qualified tax consultant or contact ZIMRA directly via their contact centre.
