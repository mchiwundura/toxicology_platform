The World Health Organization (WHO) initiated a year-long campaign starting on World Health Day 2025 to enhance maternal and newborn health globally.

The campaign aims to end preventable maternal and newborn deaths and prioritise the long-term health and well-being of women. WHO is organising global events to raise awareness and share information about improving survival rates and health outcomes for mothers and babies.

World Health Day 2025, observed on 7 April, focused on the theme "Healthy Beginnings, Hopeful Futures", emphasising maternal and newborn health.

## Five key facts about the 2025 observance

### 1. Year-long campaign

The campaign runs throughout the year, not just on World Health Day itself, to sustain attention on maternal and newborn health.

### 2. Goals of the campaign

The campaign supports WHO's aim of reducing the global maternal mortality rate to less than 70 per 100,000 live births by 2030, in line with the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goal 3.

### 3. Global events

WHO is organising events around the world to raise awareness and share information about improving survival rates and health outcomes for mothers and babies.

### 4. Public participation encouraged

Individuals are encouraged to participate by spreading awareness using hashtags like #HopefulFutures and #HealthForAll, attending events, donating, and sharing personal experiences related to maternal and newborn health.

### 5. Alignment with the Sustainable Development Goals

These initiatives underscore the global commitment to improving health outcomes for mothers and newborns, laying the foundation for healthier families and communities worldwide.
